Factchecked

Claims made this week in parliament, in ads, and on morning television.

✕False

“Australians pay the highest gas prices in the world.”

Radio interview, Tuesday

We rank ninth among OECD importers on the same measure the speaker cited, and third among exporters. Both facts are unflattering — neither is the claim made.

Checked by Hamish Vale

!Misleading

“Dental has never been part of Medicare.”

Doorstop, Wednesday

True of adult general dental. Not true of the child benefits schedule, which has covered basic dental since 2014 and which the same speaker voted to extend.

Checked by Owen Castellan

✓True

“The consultation closed before most councils were notified.”

Senate estimates, Thursday

Confirmed by the department's own timeline, tabled under questioning. 31 of 47 councils were notified after the closing date.
